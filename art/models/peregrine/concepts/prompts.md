## Shared text

Paint concept art of the Peregrine: a fast, light air-superiority fighter for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

Its build shows what it does. It is a small one-seat interceptor that hunts other flyers, slim and light: about as long as two space Rangers from the attached image painted.jpg lying end to end, and much thinner than a gunship. A long needle nose. Under each wing hangs one slim missile rail carrying two small missiles pointing forward. Twin tail fins canted outward. One big round engine bell at the back with a glowing cold blue-white exhaust. The pilot sits sealed under a single dark smoked bubble canopy far forward on the nose: no face or person is visible. Small red beacon lights on the wing tips.

It belongs to the same army as the space Ranger in painted.jpg: worn silver-grey metal, crisp dark outlines around every plate, panel lines, bolts, scratches, chipped edges and grime, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style. Its team colour is deep blue on the main armour plates, with glowing cyan-blue light strips like the Ranger's, on dark gunmetal.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold, sharp silhouette built from big, simple shapes, the wings, the two missile rails and the engine clearly visible from above.

Show the whole fighter in level flight, not firing, from a three-quarter front view, a little from above, on a plain light grey background, in soft, even light. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **dart**: a needle dart. A very long thin fuselage like a javelin, short sharply forward-swept wings set far back, the canopy a small bead near the nose tip, a big engine bell much wider than the thin fuselage.
2. **swallow**: a swallow. Long, slender forward-swept wings with pointed tips, a short body, the twin fins long and forked like a swallow's tail, the missile rails tucked close under the wing roots.
3. **racer**: an air-race plane. A polished, streamlined teardrop fuselage, a big round chrome engine bell, slim forward-swept wings with racing stripes in deep blue, the bubble canopy raised high on the nose.
4. **blade**: a flat blade. A thin, flat, knife-edged body that blends into the forward-swept wings, the nose a long sharp point, the twin fins small and sharply canted, the engine bell set into the flat tail.

## Pick (2026-10-05)

The user chose **dart** (`peregrine-c1-dart.jpg`): the model follows it, including what it changed from the brief (two engines side by side instead of one bell, wings swept back). Its sound should recall the Wraith Darts of Stargate Atlantis: an eerie, rising organic shriek.
