# Prospector concept prompts (round 1, 2026-10-01)

Each prompt goes to Grok with `painted.jpg` (the Ranger's turnaround, `art/models/ranger/painted.jpg`) attached, one after another in one chat. The result is saved as `c1-<slug>.jpg` in this folder. The shared text is the same in every prompt; only the design direction changes.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of a Prospector: a one-pilot mining and construction walker for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

Its body shows its four jobs. The right arm ends in a heavy mining drill. The left arm ends in a gripper that carries a glowing blue mineral crystal home. A welding torch sits on the drill arm for building and repairs. A pilot sits inside, the helmeted head visible.

It belongs to the same army as the space Ranger in the attached image painted.jpg: worn metal, crisp dark outlines around every plate, panel lines, bolts, scratches, chipped edges and grime, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style. Its team colour is deep blue on the main armour plates, with cyan-blue lights.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold, chunky silhouette built from big, simple shapes.

Show the whole machine from a three-quarter front view, a little from above, on a plain light grey background, in soft, even light. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **excavator**: heavy construction equipment that learned to walk. A squat operator's cab like an excavator's with a roll cage over the pilot, hydraulic rams on every joint, yellow-and-black hazard stripes on the edges, twin exhaust pipes and a big counterweight on the back.
2. **diver**: an old deep-sea diving suit grown to machine size. A round, riveted cockpit bell with three round portholes, the pilot's face behind the front one; heavy weighted boots, air hoses looping from a tank on the back to the bell, a lamp on the bell's crown.
3. **gorilla**: a hunched, gorilla-like frame. Short, bowed legs, a huge rounded back, very long, thick arms that reach the ground, the drill and the gripper as its fists; the pilot in a small glass bubble set low in the chest.
4. **strider**: reverse-jointed, bird-like legs carrying a boxy cab like a forklift's, leaning forward; the pilot behind a wide wraparound windscreen; the drill on a long boom under the cab and the gripper on a second boom beside it.
5. **frontier**: a ramshackle frontier miner's rig. Mismatched patched plates welded over a pipe frame, a cluster of work lamps on the roof, loose cables, a tarp lashed on the back, soot and oil stains; the pilot in an open seat behind a steel mesh guard.
6. **atomic**: 1950s atomic-age industry. A round, riveted fuselage like a boiler, a clear bubble canopy over the pilot, chrome trim bands, round gauges on the chest, stubby fins, heavy rounded feet: an old rocket-age design made rugged by years of mining.

# Round 2 (2026-10-01): the diver as a pressure-hull mining suit

The user liked the diver (c1-diver.jpg) but it read as underwater. Round 2 keeps its sealed bell and turns the diving cues into life support for airless, toxic worlds. Each prompt goes with `painted.jpg` and `c1-diver.jpg` attached; the results are `c2-<slug>.jpg`.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of a Prospector: a one-pilot mining and construction walker for a sci-fi real-time strategy game, built as a sealed pressure hull for airless, toxic alien worlds. It develops the design in the attached image c1-diver.jpg. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

The hull keeps the pilot alive outside: a round, riveted bell, small, thick round viewports ringed with cyan seal lights, the pilot's helmeted face behind the front one, air scrubber canisters on the back with hoses running to the bell, a miner's headlamp on the crown, warning stencils and pressure gauges on the hull. The trim is gunmetal and deep blue, and rock dust is caked on the legs and the drill.

Its body shows its four jobs. The right arm ends in a heavy mining drill. The left arm ends in a gripper that carries a glowing blue mineral crystal home. A welding torch sits on the drill arm for building and repairs.

It belongs to the same army as the space Ranger in the attached image painted.jpg: worn metal, crisp dark outlines around every plate, panel lines, bolts, scratches, chipped edges and grime, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style. Its team colour is deep blue on the main armour plates, with cyan-blue lights.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold, chunky silhouette built from big, simple shapes.

Show the whole machine from a three-quarter front view, a little from above, on a plain light grey background, in soft, even light. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **pressure**: the build of c1-diver.jpg, a big bell on sturdy legs, with every brass part turned to gunmetal and deep blue.
2. **hunched**: the bell set on a heavier, hunched frame: a huge rounded back, very long, thick arms that reach the ground, short bowed legs, the bell low between the shoulders.
3. **dome**: the bell's upper half is a clear glass dome over the pilot, framed by riveted steel ribs, with a cluster of round gauges on the chest below it.
4. **crab**: a squat, wide stance: short, thick legs set far apart, a wide, flattened bell, broad shoulders, low to the ground like a crab.

Keep everything else exactly as in c2-pressure.jpg: the round riveted bell in deep blue, the headlamp on the crown, the hoses and canisters, the gripper with the glowing blue crystal, the drill and welding torch, the rock dust, the plain light grey background. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **porthole**: The big round porthole holds thick, dark smoked armoured glass, glossy and opaque, with a soft cyan glow from the instruments behind it and the cyan seal light ringing its rim.
2. **visor**: The round porthole becomes a wide horizontal visor slit across the bell's front, filled with dark smoked armoured glass that glows faintly cyan from inside, framed by a heavy riveted steel brow.
