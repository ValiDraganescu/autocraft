# Kestrel concept prompts (round 1, 2026-10-04)

Each prompt goes to Grok in its own new chat with `painted.jpg` (the Ranger's turnaround, `art/models/ranger/painted.jpg`) attached. The result is saved as `c1-<slug>.jpg` in this folder; each prompt is also written out as `prompt.<slug>.txt`. The shared text describes the build and keeps the crew hidden. The game needs a body that banks and pitches as one, two vectored engine nacelles (they tilt in flight, each with a glowing exhaust), one rocket pod under each wing whose bores face straight forward (each pod kicks back on its rail when it fires), and red beacons on the wing tips.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of the Kestrel: a fast rocket gunship for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

Its build shows what it does. It is a small, fast two-seat attack flyer that hovers low and hunts ground targets, about as long as three space Rangers from the attached image painted.jpg lying end to end. It hovers on two vectored jet engine nacelles, one each side of the hull, their glowing orange exhausts at the back. Under each short wing hangs one fat rocket pod: a drum of six rocket tubes, the six round bores facing straight forward. The crew sits sealed inside a dark smoked canopy at the front: no face or person is visible. Small red beacon lights on the wing tips.

It belongs to the same army as the space Ranger in painted.jpg: worn silver-grey metal, crisp dark outlines around every plate, panel lines, bolts, scratches, chipped edges and grime, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style. Its team colour is deep blue on the main armour plates, with glowing cyan-blue light strips like the Ranger's, on dark gunmetal.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold silhouette built from big, simple shapes, the two rocket pods and the two engines clearly visible from above.

Show the whole gunship hovering, not firing, from a three-quarter front view, a little from above, on a plain light grey background, in soft, even light. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **gunship**: the classic attack gunship. A slim, pointed armoured hull with a stepped tandem canopy, short straight stub wings, the engine nacelles at the wing roots, and twin tail booms ending in canted fins joined by a blue tailplane.
2. **falcon**: a diving falcon. A sharp hooked nose like a beak over the canopy, wings swept forward and down like a stooping bird of prey's, the engine nacelles tucked under the wing roots like folded legs, and a fanned, notched tail of three short fins.
3. **wasp**: a wasp. A narrow waisted hull in two armoured segments, a bulbous rear segment holding the engines side by side, short thin wings set high, the rocket pods hanging low under them like a wasp's legs carrying cargo, and a short sting-like tail spike.
4. **tiltjet**: a tilt-jet. A boxy armoured hull with a wide blunt canopy, long straight wings, and a big round engine nacelle at each wing tip that swivels to point down for hovering, the rocket pods on short pylons under the wings between the hull and the nacelles, and a single tall fin.
5. **manta**: a manta ray. A wide, flat, rounded delta body with the canopy as a raised blister in the middle, the wings blending smoothly into the body, the engine nacelles half buried in the rear edge, the rocket pods slung flat under the body's edges, and a short thin tail.
6. **hotrod**: a hot-rod racer of the sky. A long, low, wedge-shaped hull, two huge chrome-ringed engine nacelles mounted high on the flanks behind the canopy like a dragster's, stubby swept wings carrying the rocket pods, and a small raised spoiler fin at the back.
