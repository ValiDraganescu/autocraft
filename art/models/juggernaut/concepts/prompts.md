# Juggernaut concept prompts (round 1, 2026-10-01)

Each prompt goes to Grok in its own new chat with `painted.jpg` (the Ranger's turnaround, `art/models/ranger/painted.jpg`) attached. The result is saved as `c1-<slug>.jpg` in this folder; each prompt is also written out as `prompt.<slug>.txt`. The shared text is the same in every prompt; only the design direction changes. It describes the body itself (the Comet rounds showed Grok follows a described build, not an adjective), and keeps the face hidden (the Prospector rounds showed a hidden face models well). The game needs the forearms to be the launchers: they pivot at the elbow, hang at ease and come up level to fire.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of a Juggernaut: a heavy assault trooper for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

Its body shows what it does. It is massive and slow, about twice the bulk of the space Ranger in painted.jpg: huge shoulders, a deep chest, thick legs on heavy boots. It has no hands: each forearm is a Punisher grenade launcher, a heavy armoured block with a wide bore at the end, so it fires with both arms at once. Its face is hidden behind a helmet with two glowing orange eyes. It wears the heaviest armour of its army: it is a walking tank.

It belongs to the same army as the space Ranger in the attached image painted.jpg: worn metal, crisp dark outlines around every plate, panel lines, bolts, scratches, chipped edges and grime, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style. Its team colour is deep blue on the main armour plates.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold silhouette built from big, simple shapes, the two launchers clearly visible.

Show the whole trooper standing, launchers lowered, from a three-quarter front view, a little from above, on a plain light grey background, in soft, even light. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **juggernaut**: the classic heavy trooper. Two enormous rounded shoulder pads rise above a small helmet sunk deep in a high armoured collar, the launchers are long drum-fed tubes, exhaust stacks rise from a backpack.
2. **gorilla**: a hunched brute like a silverback. Long, massive arms that reach almost to the ground, a hump of armour on the back, short bowed legs, a low helmet pushed forward between the shoulders.
3. **siegeknight**: a medieval siege knight in sci-fi plate. A great helm with a narrow eye slit, layered plates over the hips like tassets, a heavy gorget, launchers shaped like short mortars with flared muzzles.
4. **bunker**: a walking bunker. Flat, slab-sided armour like poured concrete and steel, a helmet with a narrow gun-slit visor, sandbag-like padded plates on the shoulders, square launchers with armoured shutters at the bores.
5. **demolition**: a mining demolition crew suit sent to war. Hazard-striped edges, a round reinforced helmet with a grilled face plate, belts of grenades across the chest, revolver-drum magazines on the launchers, a big fuel and ammo pack on the back.
6. **warden**: a prison riot enforcer. Thick padded armour under heavy plates, a gas-mask helmet with two round filters and glowing eyes, wide shoulder shields, launchers like riot grenade guns with ribbed barrels.
