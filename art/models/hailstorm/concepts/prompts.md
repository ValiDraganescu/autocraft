# Hailstorm concept prompts (round 1, 2026-10-04)

The Hailstorm is new: a mobile anti-air half-track trained at the Foundry, a flak mount that only shoots at the sky (a dedicated anti-air role). Each prompt goes to Grok in its own new chat with the Longbow's turnaround `painted.jpg` (`art/models/longbow/painted.jpg`) attached, so it stays in the Longbow's and Firefly's army. The result is saved as `c1-<slug>.jpg`; each prompt is written out as `prompt.<slug>.txt`. The game needs: two big front wheels that steer and roll, a track unit on each side behind them (a half-track), a turret that turns all the way round, a gun cradle that raises the barrels, four short autocannon barrels that fire in pairs, and a small tracking radar on the turret's back that spins. Each image shows the vehicle twice (the Longbow's two-pose image worked): at rest, and firing at the sky.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of a Hailstorm: a mobile anti-air flak vehicle for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

Its build shows what it does. It is an armoured half-track, a little smaller than the Longbow in the attached image painted.jpg: about two thirds of its length, its turret top about as high as that tank's turret roof. Two big armoured wheels at the front, one each side, and a heavy rubber track unit on each side behind them. On its back a flak turret that turns all the way round, carrying exactly four short, thick autocannon barrels side by side in two pairs on a raised gun cradle, and a small round radar dish on a short mast at the turret's back. It only shoots up at the sky: the barrels point forward and a little up at rest and swing steeply up to fire. The crew is sealed inside, out of sight: no face or person is visible.

It belongs to the same army as the Longbow in painted.jpg: worn metal, crisp dark outlines around every plate, panel lines, bolts, scratches, chipped edges and grime, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style. Its team colour is deep blue on the main armour plates, on dark gunmetal, with a few small amber lights.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold silhouette built from big, simple shapes, the four barrels, the turret and the wheels clearly visible.

Show the same vehicle twice, side by side, both from a three-quarter front view, a little from above: on the left at rest, the barrels low and pointing forward; on the right firing, the turret turned a little, the four barrels pitched steeply up at the sky with bright muzzle flashes and small dark flak bursts above. A plain light grey background, in soft, even light. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **porcupine**: a porcupine. A low, humped armoured body bristling with short spiked armour quills along its back and sides, the turret riding on the hump, the four barrels like the thickest quills.
2. **armadillo**: an armadillo. A long rounded body covered in overlapping banded armour plates across its back, a pointed armoured snout at the front between the wheels, and the turret set into the bands like a raised shell segment.
3. **boar**: a wild boar. A heavy, wedge-shaped front with two curved tusk-like ram plates low between the front wheels, high muscular shoulders of armour, and a bristly ridge of plates running back to the turret.
4. **tortoise**: a tortoise. A high, domed, segmented shell for the turret, the four barrels poking out of its front like a neck, the hull low and flat beneath it with stubby armoured skirts over the wheels and tracks.
5. **shilka**: a classic self-propelled anti-air gun. A boxy, utilitarian half-track with a sloped armoured cab at the front, a flat bed behind, a squat square turret with the four barrels in two stacked pairs and ammunition drums on its sides.
6. **owl**: an owl. A compact, upright body with a broad, round-headed turret, two big round radar eyes on its face that look up at the sky, feather-like layered armour plates on its sides, and the barrels set under the eyes like a beak.
