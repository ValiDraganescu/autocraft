# Firefly concept prompts (round 1, 2026-10-01)

Each prompt goes to Grok in its own new chat with `painted.jpg` (the Ranger's turnaround, `art/models/ranger/painted.jpg`) attached. The result is saved as `c1-<slug>.jpg` in this folder; each prompt is also written out as `prompt.<slug>.txt`. The shared text describes the build (the Comet rounds: Grok follows a described build, not an adjective) and keeps the driver hidden. The game needs four wheels (the front pair steering), a body on its suspension, and one flamethrower whose nozzle points straight ahead along the hull (a driven Firefly aims with the hull), with a pilot light.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of a Firefly: a fast flamethrower raider vehicle for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

Its build shows what it does. It is a light, fast one-seat attack buggy, about as long as three space Rangers from the attached image painted.jpg lying end to end and lower than one standing: four big off-road wheels on long-travel suspension, the front pair steering, chunky treaded tyres. One flamethrower points straight ahead along the vehicle's length, its nozzle out in front of the vehicle, with a small blue pilot flame in its mouth; it is fed from heavy fuel tanks on the vehicle. The driver is sealed inside, out of sight, behind a dark smoked or glowing amber canopy: no face or person is visible. Exhaust stacks at the back.

It belongs to the same army as the space Ranger in painted.jpg: worn metal, crisp dark outlines around every plate, panel lines, bolts, scratches, chipped edges and grime, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style. Its team colour is deep blue on the main armour plates, on dark gunmetal.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold silhouette built from big, simple shapes, the flamethrower and the four wheels clearly visible.

Show the whole vehicle parked, flamethrower not firing, from a three-quarter front view, a little from above, on a plain light grey background, in soft, even light. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **raider**: the classic raider buggy. A long, low, pointed hood split down the middle runs forward between the front wheels on bare wishbone arms, a raised armoured hump over the big rear wheels, and the flamethrower arches over the canopy from a post on the rear deck.
2. **hotrod**: a hot-rod dragster. Huge rear wheels and smaller front wheels set far forward on long exposed suspension arms, an open supercharged engine block with chrome intake and exhaust stacks behind the cockpit, and the flamethrower running along the spine to a nozzle over the nose.
3. **trophy**: a desert trophy truck. A wide, tall-riding body inside a heavy roll cage, very long suspension travel with coil-over shocks, spare fuel drums strapped behind the cockpit, and the flamethrower mounted on top of the roll cage.
4. **scorpion**: a scorpion. A low, segmented armoured body between wide-set wheels in armoured pods like claws, and the flamethrower is a jointed tail that arches up over the back and points forward over the cockpit, its fuel tank as the tail's bulb.
5. **napalm**: an industrial napalm tanker. The body is one big rounded fuel tank with hazard-striped bands and pressure gauges, a small armoured cab at the front, and the flamethrower nozzle on a short armoured boom low between the front wheels.
6. **hornet**: a wedge-shaped armoured speedster. A flat, angular wedge body low to the ground, each wheel inside its own armoured pod on a short arm, two fuel tanks along the flanks like a jet's intakes, and the flamethrower a long barrel under the nose.
