# Comet concept prompts (round 1, 2026-10-01)

Each prompt goes to Grok with `painted.jpg` (the Ranger's turnaround, `art/models/ranger/painted.jpg`) attached, one after another in one chat. The result is saved as `c1-<slug>.jpg` in this folder. The shared text is the same in every prompt; only the design direction changes. The Prospector rounds showed that a hidden face models well and a visible one does not, so every direction keeps the face behind a mask.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of a Comet: a fast, lightly armoured raider soldier for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

Its body shows what it does. A jetpack on its back lets it leap up cliffs, with two nozzles that blast fire downward. It fires two heavy pistols, one in each hand. Its face is hidden behind a mask with two glowing eyes. It wears light armour over its fatigues: it is fast, not tough.

It belongs to the same army as the space Ranger in the attached image painted.jpg: worn metal, crisp dark outlines around every plate, panel lines, bolts, scratches, chipped edges and grime, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style. Its team colour is deep blue on the main armour plates.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold silhouette built from big, simple shapes, the jetpack clearly visible.

Show the whole soldier standing, pistols lowered, from a three-quarter front view, a little from above, on a plain light grey background, in soft, even light. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **outlaw**: a frontier desperado. A long, tattered duster coat split at the back for the jetpack, a wide-brimmed hat over a gas mask, bandoliers across the chest, long-barrelled revolver-like pistols, a battered twin-tank jetpack.
2. **rocketeer**: 1950s rocket-age adventure serial. A bullet-shaped twin rocket pack with tail fins and chrome trim bands, a smooth rounded helmet with a narrow glowing eye slit and a crest fin, a leather flight jacket over armour, chunky ray-gun pistols.
3. **beetle**: an insect-like raider. The jetpack folds over the back like a beetle's wing cases and opens for flight, segmented armour on a lean body, a smooth helmet with two big round glowing eyes like an insect's, reverse-jointed leg braces for leaping.
4. **scavenger**: a junkyard punk. A jetpack built from mismatched scrap and engine parts, a skull spray-painted on the mask, a tall fin crest like a mohawk, chains, torn cloth, spikes on the shoulders, sawn-off pistols.
5. **convict**: a prison raider, freed for war. A numbered jumpsuit under armour plates, a heavy steel collar with a blinking light, a riveted muzzle-like mask with glowing eyes, broken shackles on the wrists, a crude, oversized industrial jetpack welded together.
6. **stealth**: a sleek night commando. Matte dark armour hugging a slim body, low-profile ducted-fan jets on the back, a smooth faceless helmet with two thin glowing eye slits, compact angular pistols, minimal and sharp.

# Round 2 (2026-10-01): the beetle, slim

The user went on with the recommended beetle (c1-beetle.jpg). Every round-1 figure came back as heavy as a Ranger, so round 2 describes the slim body itself, part by part, and leaves `c1-beetle.jpg` out (an attached concept gets its build copied). Each prompt goes with `painted.jpg` only; the results are `c2-<slug>.jpg`.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of a Comet: a fast raider soldier for a sci-fi real-time strategy game, designed like a beetle. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

The body is slim and wiry, like a sprinter's: long thin legs, a narrow waist, a narrow chest, thin arms, about half the bulk of the space Ranger in painted.jpg. It wears dark, tight fatigues with only small, segmented armour plates on the shins, thighs, forearms, shoulders and chest. A smooth, rounded helmet with two big, round glowing eyes like an insect's hides the face completely. On its back, two curved armoured wing cases close over the jetpack like a beetle's shell, with two thruster nozzles under them pointing down. It holds a heavy pistol in each hand.

It belongs to the same army as the space Ranger in the attached image painted.jpg: worn metal, crisp dark outlines around every plate, panel lines, bolts, scratches, chipped edges and grime, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style. Its team colour is deep blue on the wing cases and the armour plates.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold silhouette built from big, simple shapes, the wing cases clearly visible.

Show the whole soldier standing, pistols lowered, from a three-quarter front view, a little from above, on a plain light grey background, in soft, even light. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **sprinter**: the plain design as described, lean and quick, the wing cases closed smooth and glossy.
2. **leap**: show it twice side by side: on the left standing at rest, wing cases closed; on the right in mid-leap, the wing cases swung open sideways like a beetle taking off, both nozzles blasting fire downward, knees tucked, pistols out.
3. **rhino**: a rhinoceros beetle. A single curved horn rises from the helmet's crown and forehead, and the wing cases are tall and domed.
4. **stag**: a stag beetle. Two pincer-shaped respirator filters curve forward from the helmet's jaw like mandibles, and the wing cases are long and narrow.
5. **scarab**: a scarab. The wing cases are broad and round with a glowing seam down the middle where they meet, and the helmet is a low, wide dome.
