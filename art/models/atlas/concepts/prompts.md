## Shared text

Paint concept art of the Atlas: a huge, heavy two-legged assault walker for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

Its build shows what it does. It is the army's biggest war machine, about four times as tall as the space Ranger in the attached image painted.jpg and many times its bulk. Two strong reverse-jointed legs, knees bending backward like a bird's, each ending in a broad three-toed foot. A squat, heavily armoured torso sits on the hips. On its shoulders sit two long, heavy siege cannons pointing forward. The crew sits sealed behind a narrow dark smoked cockpit slit at the front of the torso: no face or person is visible. A round searchlight under the cockpit slit. Amber warning lamps on the knees. Heat vents with glowing orange slats on the back.

It belongs to the same army as the space Ranger in painted.jpg: worn silver-grey metal, crisp dark outlines around every plate, panel lines, bolts, scratches, chipped edges and grime, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style. Its team colour is deep blue on the main armour plates, with glowing cyan-blue light strips like the Ranger's, on dark gunmetal.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold, chunky silhouette built from big, simple shapes, the two cannons clearly visible from above.

Show it twice side by side on a plain light grey background, in soft, even light, both from a three-quarter front view a little from above: on the left standing at rest, on the right raising one foot high for a ground-shaking stomp. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **mech**: a classic heavy mech. A wide, boxy torso like an armoured bunker with sloped front plates, thick armoured thighs, hydraulic rams on every joint, the cannons long and straight on heavy shoulder mounts.
2. **ostrich**: a tall bird-like walker. Long powerful legs, a compact rounded torso carried high and leaning forward, the cannons slung low along the torso's sides like folded wings, a short armoured tail as a counterweight.
3. **foundry**: industrial heavy machinery. A torso like a riveted boiler with hazard-striped edges and exhaust stacks, chunky piston legs, the cannons like steel mill rollers in big ring mounts, chains and cable bundles.
4. **titan**: an ancient armoured giant. Massive layered shoulder plates like a knight's pauldrons, a narrow waist, a broad chest, heavy stomping feet, the cannons mounted on top of the pauldrons like twin towers.
