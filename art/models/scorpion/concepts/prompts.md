## Shared text

Paint concept art of the Scorpion: a small burrowing mine robot for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

Its build shows what it does. It is a low six-legged crawler, about as long as the space Ranger in the attached image painted.jpg is tall, and much lower: it walks close to the ground on six jointed legs. Its back is covered by overlapping armoured plates in segments. A raised, segmented tail curls over its back and ends in a single heavy launcher with one round bore and a red lamp beside it. No crew: it is a robot, with a small cluster of glowing sensor eyes at the front.

It belongs to the same army as the space Ranger in painted.jpg: worn silver-grey metal, crisp dark outlines around every plate, panel lines, bolts, scratches, chipped edges and grime, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style. Its team colour is deep blue on the main armour plates, with glowing cyan-blue light strips like the Ranger's, on dark gunmetal.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold silhouette built from big, simple shapes, the back plates and the tail clearly visible from above.

Show it twice side by side on a plain light grey background, in soft, even light, both from a three-quarter front view a little from above: on the left walking, on the right half buried in a mound of loose soil, only its back plates and its raised tail showing, the tail aimed forward. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **scorpion**: a true scorpion. A flat oval body, two small pincers folded at the front, the tail long and arched high in a classic scorpion curve.
2. **trilobite**: an armoured trilobite. A wide, flat, rounded shell of many overlapping plates that hides the legs from above, the tail short and thick, rising from the shell's rear edge.
3. **tick**: a squat tick-like mine. A round, domed, heavy body like a sea mine with plates, short stubby legs, the tail a short stubby periscope-like launcher on top.
4. **mantis**: a mantis-like drill bug. A narrow body, front legs shaped like digging blades, a drill-tipped nose for burrowing, the tail thin and whip-like with the launcher at the tip.

## Pick (2026-10-05)

The user chose **scorpion** (`scorpion-c1-scorpion.jpg`). The buried pose is modelled by sinking the body into a soil mound, not taken from the concept (which stands on the heap).
