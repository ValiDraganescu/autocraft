# Sentinel concept prompts (round 1, 2026-10-04)

Each prompt goes to Grok (grok.com, driven from Chrome by the agent; the user
was offline) in a new chat, with `base.jpg` attached: the Bastion, Hab Dome
and Garrison as the game renders them (`Autocraft preview`), so the tower
stays in the same base. The result is saved as `c1-<slug>.jpg`; each exact
prompt is in `prompt.<slug>.txt`.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of the Sentinel: a compact anti-air missile tower for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

It stands on a square 2 by 2 pad, the same small footprint as the Hab Dome in the attached image base.jpg, and it shoots at aircraft only. On top sits a turning head that carries two missile pods, one on each side, angled up at the sky, with the noses of the missiles showing in their tubes. A small red beacon light sits on a short mast, and a band of yellow-orange working lights glows round the tower.

It belongs to the same base as the three buildings in base.jpg: silver-grey steel plates with panel lines, rivets and wear, a dark gunmetal plinth with yellow-and-black hazard stripes on its edge, deep blue team-colour trim, crisp dark outlines round every plate, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold, chunky silhouette built from big, simple shapes, the two missile pods the first thing you see.

Show the whole tower from a three-quarter front view, from above, on a plain light grey background, in soft, even light. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **pillbox**: a low, heavy octagonal armoured base like the Bastion's, and on it a squat round turret drum with a missile pod bolted to each flank.
2. **radar**: a slim, tall tower; the head carries a round radar dish on its back between the two pods, and the pods sit high on short arms.
3. **yoke**: a square armoured bunker base with a big turntable on its roof; the head is a U-shaped yoke holding two big box launchers, each a grid of six square tube hatches.
4. **tripod**: three splayed steel legs on the pad hold a turret ring above an open frame, ammunition crates and cables underneath, a frontier-built rig.
5. **dome**: a half-buried armoured dome, the head rising out of a slot in its top, two drum magazines of missiles on either side of the head.
6. **rails**: an open launcher: two long missiles lie fully visible on rails on each side of the head, white bodies with red tips, behind a riveted blast shield.
