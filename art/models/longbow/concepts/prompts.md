# Longbow concept prompts (round 1, 2026-10-01)

Each prompt goes to Grok in its own new chat with `painted.jpg` (the Ranger's turnaround, `art/models/ranger/painted.jpg`) attached. The result is saved as `c1-<slug>.jpg` in this folder; each prompt is also written out as `prompt.<slug>.txt`. The game needs four track pods on swing-out legs, clawed struts that plant in siege mode, a hull that kneels, a turret that turns all the way round, and one gun: the siege cannon, packed short and flat in travel mode (it fires like that too) and raised, pitched up and run out to full length sieged. Each image shows both modes side by side (the Comet's two-pose image worked).

## Shared text

Paint concept art of a Longbow: a heavy artillery tank for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

Its build shows what it does. It is a heavy armoured tank, about as long as four space Rangers from the attached image painted.jpg lying end to end, its turret roof about as high as one standing. It drives on four separate armoured track pods, one at each corner, each on a short swing-out leg. A turret on top turns all the way round. The turret has exactly one gun: a long siege cannon that telescopes. In travel mode the cannon is packed: slid in to half its length and lying flat along the turret roof, pointing forward; the tank has no other barrels. In siege mode the track pods have swung out wide, a heavy clawed strut under each pod is planted in the ground, the hull has knelt low, and the cannon has risen, pitched up toward the sky and run out to its full length, with green lock lights along it. The crew is sealed inside, out of sight: no face or person is visible.

It belongs to the same army as the space Ranger in painted.jpg: worn metal, crisp dark outlines around every plate, panel lines, bolts, scratches, chipped edges and grime, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style. Its team colour is deep blue on the main armour plates, on dark gunmetal.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold silhouette built from big, simple shapes, the four track pods and the cannon clearly visible.

Show the same tank twice, side by side, both from a three-quarter front view, a little from above: on the left in travel mode, on the right in siege mode. A plain light grey background, in soft, even light. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **artillery**: the classic tracked artillery tank. A low, angular hull with a raked front plate, a big faceted turret with light armoured cheek towers either side, and the cannon's breech a heavy block at the turret's rear.
2. **crab**: an armoured crab. A wide, flat, rounded carapace for the hull and turret, the four track pods tucked under its edge like a crab's legs and swung out wide like them in siege mode, and the cannon like one long pincer arm.
3. **howitzer**: an industrial self-propelled howitzer. A boxy, utilitarian hull with hazard stripes, the cannon in a heavy cradle between two big hydraulic recoil cylinders, and hydraulic outrigger struts with round ground pads.
4. **railgun**: an energy railgun platform. A sleek, angular hull, the cannon two parallel rails with glowing blue coils between them that slide out lengthwise, and cooling fins on the turret.
5. **fortress**: a squat armoured fortress. A low, heavy, wedge-shaped hull with thick skirts over the track pods, a round domed turret like a bunker, and the cannon telescoping out of the dome in three sections.
6. **rhino**: a rhinoceros. A massive, hunched armoured hull with a thick horn-like ram plate at the front, overlapping armour plates like a hide, and the cannon riding on the hump of its back.
