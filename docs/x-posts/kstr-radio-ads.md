## Post

Ringshadow is an RTS in Unreal Engine 5 where you can take over any unit and play it like a shooter. The music plays as a radio station, KSTR 88.7 Stardust: songs made with Suno, with in-world ads between them.

Quicksilver Cola is pumped from Metallic Hydrogen wells and is not a beverage. Opal Glow face scrub side effects include levitation and being mined.

The [ and ] keys vote a song up or down. The votes go to a log, and I'll write the next batch of songs from them. Next on the air, maybe: talk shows about the lore of Ashfall Reach.
