## Post

Autocraft is an RTS where an AI commander runs your army, and you can take over any of your units and play it like a shooter.

In the clip I click a Ranger on the map. The camera dives from the top-down view to the Ranger's own eyes in 1.2 seconds, and it walks out in first person.

From there it plays like a shooter: WASD walks, the mouse aims, a click fires. Esc hands the Ranger back to the AI commander.

It works the same on all twelve kinds of unit, each with its own cockpit and weapon.
